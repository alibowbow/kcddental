## Verified takeaways

- In the KDCA 2024 child oral health survey, the permanent-tooth caries experience rate among 12-year-old children was 60.3%.
- The mean number of caries-experienced permanent teeth was about 1.9 per child.
- 10-year trend of the 12-year-old caries experience rate: 2010 60.5%, 2012 57.3%, 2015 54.6%, 2018 56.4%, 2021-2022 58.4%, 2024 60.3% (recent uptick).
- Figures are reported consistently across major Korean news outlets citing the KDCA survey.

## Scope note

- These are Korea-specific child survey figures for `K02`; keep separate from WHO global figures.
- The 60.3% is a caries *experience* rate (DMFT-based), not a point-prevalence of active decay.
- Do not extrapolate child figures to adults or to other K-codes.
