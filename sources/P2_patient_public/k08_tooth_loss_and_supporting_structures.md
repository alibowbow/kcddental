## Verified takeaways

- Periodontitis is inflammation and infection of the ligaments and bone that support the teeth.
- If gum inflammation is not treated, the support structures can break down and teeth can become loose.
- Periodontitis is a primary cause of tooth loss in adults.
- Common symptoms include bleeding gums, gum recession, loose teeth, and shifting teeth.
- Regular brushing, flossing, and dental cleaning reduce risk.
