## Verified takeaways

- K00.5 is a hereditary tooth-structure bucket, so the main safe themes are weak enamel, defective dentin, discoloration, and rapid wear.
- Amelogenesis imperfecta is a group of disorders where enamel does not form properly, and teeth may be small, discolored, pitted, grooved, weak, or prone to cavities and breakage.
- Dentinogenesis imperfecta causes discolored, translucent, weaker teeth with soft dentin and a tendency to wear, break, and be lost.
- Dentinogenesis imperfecta type I can occur as part of osteogenesis imperfecta, so systemic bone disease can be relevant to the tooth-structure story.

## Scope note

- Keep this note on inherited structure defects, not on eruption timing or jaw alignment.
- It should not absorb non-hereditary discoloration, trauma, or simple wear unless the link is explicitly genetic.
