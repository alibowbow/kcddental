## Verified takeaways

- TMD is a group of disorders affecting the jaw joint and chewing muscles.
- Common symptoms include jaw pain, stiffness, limited movement, and painful clicking or popping.
- There is no single standard test; diagnosis relies on history, exam, and sometimes imaging.
- Conservative care comes first, and bite-changing irreversible treatments should be approached carefully.
