## Verified takeaways

- Tongue pain or burning can come from acidic foods, toothpaste ingredients, mouthwash, medications, thrush, tongue injury, or tongue sores.
- Intense tongue or whole-mouth pain can be caused by burning mouth syndrome.
- When tongue discomfort is not caused by infection, the first step is often removing the trigger, such as irritating foods or products.
- Another MSD tongue article notes that ulcers or bumps on the tongue can also come from irritation, viral infection, aphthous ulcers, bacterial infection, early syphilis, immune disease, or biting injury.
- A one-sided tongue bump or unexplained red or white tongue lesion should be examined rather than assumed to be harmless.

## Scope note

- This note is intentionally limited to common patient-visible symptoms and warning signs.
- It is safe for future `lib/data` mapping to use for symptoms, self-care, and red-flag guidance.
- It should not be treated as a definitive differential for every tongue lesion.
