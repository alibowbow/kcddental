## Verified takeaways

- Gingival recession means the gum margin moves away from the tooth and exposes the root.
- It may cause sensitivity, longer-looking teeth, and higher root-caries risk.
- It does not usually grow back on its own, but progression and symptoms can be reduced.
- First-line management is cause control and hygiene; grafting may be considered in selected cases.
