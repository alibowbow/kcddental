- year/effective date: `2019 (leaflet reference 022019)`

## Verified takeaways

- Tooth wear is a patient-facing term for loss of tooth surface over time.
- The main wear patterns are erosion, attrition, and abrasion.
- Wear can make teeth look thinner, shinier, or more sensitive.
- Acid exposure and repetitive mechanical wear are common explanations.
