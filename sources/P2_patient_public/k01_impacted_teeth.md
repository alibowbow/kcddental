## Verified takeaways

- An impacted tooth is unable to emerge properly from the gum.
- Overcrowding is a common cause, and wisdom teeth are the most common impacted teeth because the jaw often lacks room.
- Impacted teeth may become infected and may be of little use in chewing.
- Dental x-rays are used to confirm that one or more teeth have not emerged.
- Removal is a common treatment for impacted wisdom teeth.
