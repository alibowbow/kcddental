## Verified takeaways

- K00 is broad, so safe root content should stay at the level of delayed eruption, missing teeth, and structural tooth-development differences.
- Public sources support checking eruption timing, family history, associated syndromes or endocrine disorders, and dental X-rays when teeth are delayed or look abnormal.
- Some tooth-development problems make teeth weak, discolored, or unusually shaped rather than simply missing.
- Early dental follow-up matters more than trying to promise prevention for every subtype.
