## Verified takeaways

- MRONJ is bone death affecting the upper or lower jawbones.
- It can occur spontaneously or after a tooth extraction or jaw injury.
- Risk is higher after high-dose bisphosphonates or some anticancer drugs, and the condition is distinct from radiation-related osteonecrosis.
- Common symptoms include pain and pus oozing from the mouth or jaw area.
- Treatment may include antibiotics, mouth rinses, and limited debridement.

## Scope note

- This note uses MRONJ as a conservative patient-facing slice of K10.
- It does not attempt to cover every jaw cyst, tumor-like lesion, infection, or developmental jaw disorder in the umbrella code.
