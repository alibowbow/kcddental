## Verified takeaways

- K00.4 is a tooth-formation bucket, so the safe patient-facing center is delayed, absent, or unusually shaped teeth.
- MedlinePlus notes that delayed or absent tooth formation can be normal in some children, but it can also be linked to syndromes or endocrine disorders.
- Abnormally shaped teeth can be caused by multiple congenital or developmental conditions, and dental X-rays or other tests may be used when needed.
- Cleidocranial dysplasia is a useful example because it affects tooth development and can involve delayed or abnormal eruption plus other skeletal findings.

## Scope note

- Keep this note focused on formation, shape, and eruption timing.
- Do not let it drift into `K01` impacted teeth or `K07` bite alignment unless the overlap is explicitly needed for context.
