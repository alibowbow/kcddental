## Verified takeaways

- K06 can be safely summarized through three recurring patient problems: recession, gum overgrowth, and denture-related soft-tissue irritation.
- Gum recession exposes roots and often causes sensitivity; gum hyperplasia makes tissue puffy and harder to clean.
- Medication history, brushing technique, plaque control, and denture fit are high-yield review points.
- Root-level treatment can stay conservative: cause correction first, then periodontal or soft-tissue procedures if symptoms persist.
