## Verified takeaways

- K00.2 is best treated as a bucket for abnormal tooth size and shape, not as one disease.
- Abnormally shaped teeth can be linked with specific inherited or congenital conditions, and the appearance of the teeth should be interpreted with age of eruption and other dental findings.
- MedlinePlus lists examples such as microdontia, macrodontia, peg teeth, conical teeth, connate teeth, conjoined teeth, and mulberry molars.
- Some genetic conditions can produce small, peg-shaped, or malformed teeth and can also affect the jaw or other structures, so the dental shape finding should not be overgeneralized.
- Dentinogenesis imperfecta is more about defective dentin and weak teeth than a pure shape diagnosis, so it is useful mainly as a conservative supporting example rather than a blanket explanation for K00.2.
