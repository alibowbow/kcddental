## Verified takeaways

- A dentigerous cyst is a developmental odontogenic cyst that forms around an unerupted tooth.
- Many are discovered on imaging before they cause symptoms.
- Larger lesions can displace teeth or expand bone.
- Treatment usually involves oral surgery evaluation and cyst removal or decompression.
