## Verified takeaways

- Hyperdontia means having extra teeth beyond the normal tooth count.
- Extra teeth can be found incidentally or because they delay eruption, crowd nearby teeth, or interfere with alignment.
- Cleveland Clinic describes common extra-tooth locations and forms, including mesiodens, paramolars, and distomolars.
- MedlinePlus notes that extra teeth are a recognized cause of malocclusion, crowding, and abnormal bite patterns.
- Dental x-rays are important for confirming the number and location of the extra teeth.
- Treatment may be observation or removal depending on whether the teeth are impacted, crowding the bite, or causing symptoms.

## Subcode mapping

- `K00.10` fits best for extra teeth in the incisor or canine region.
- `K00.11` fits best for extra teeth in the premolar region.
- `K00.12` fits best for extra teeth in the molar region.
- `K00.19` is safest when the location is not clearly documented.

## Scope note

- This note is for the patient-facing core of extra teeth, not full orthodontic planning.
- Do not mix this with impacted teeth (`K01`) unless the record clearly says both are present.
- If the source only says the bite is crowded, keep the mapping conservative and do not overstate the cause.
