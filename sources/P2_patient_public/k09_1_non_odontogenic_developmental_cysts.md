## Verified takeaways

- Nasopalatine duct cyst is a useful patient-facing example of a non-odontogenic developmental oral cyst.
- It often appears in the anterior palate and may be asymptomatic or cause swelling, drainage, or discomfort.
- Imaging and pathology matter because it can resemble other anterior maxillary lesions.
- Oral surgery evaluation is appropriate when it is enlarging or symptomatic.
