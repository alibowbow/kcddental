## Verified takeaways

- Oral ulcers and inflammation can affect the lips and any part of the mouth, not only one fixed spot.
- Common causes include viral infection, bacterial infection, yeast overgrowth, syphilis, and immune-related disease.
- Lesions that do not heal, especially white, red, or mixed red-white areas lasting more than 2 weeks, need further evaluation.
- Persistent or painful mucosal atrophy can also be a clue to systemic disease rather than a purely local problem.
