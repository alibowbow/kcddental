## Verified takeaways

- K07 is not one disease. It combines jaw-size problems, malocclusion, tooth-position anomalies, and some TMJ-related dysfunction.
- Safe root messaging is about abnormal bite, chewing difficulty, jaw discomfort, and the need for orthodontic or TMJ-focused evaluation.
- MedlinePlus supports braces, extraction, appliance therapy, and rare jaw surgery for malocclusion.
- NIDCR supports conservative-first TMJ care and warns against irreversible bite-changing treatments as a default step.

## Scope note

- Keep K07 as an umbrella overview.
- Detailed disease logic should eventually move to subcodes like `K07.2`, `K07.3`, and `K07.6`.
