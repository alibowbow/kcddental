## Verified takeaways

- K00.7 should be treated as a normal-eruption symptom bucket, not as a general explanation for fever or other serious illness.
- MedlinePlus says teething is the growth of teeth through the gums in infants and young children, and explicitly notes that teething does not cause fever or diarrhea.
- NHS guidance supports safe symptomatic care such as teething rings, cool chewing options, and age-appropriate paracetamol or ibuprofen when needed.
- Cleveland Clinic provides a simple eruption timing chart and reinforces that eruption timing varies from child to child.

## Scope note

- This note should stay very narrow and patient-facing.
- Use it for comfort measures, timing, and red-flag warnings only.
- It should not be expanded into a diagnosis of unexplained fever, rash, diarrhea, or infection.
