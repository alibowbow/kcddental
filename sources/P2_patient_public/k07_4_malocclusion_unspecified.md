## Verified takeaways

- Malocclusion means the teeth do not fit together properly.
- It can show up as overbite, underbite, open bite, crossbite, crowding, spacing, or other abnormal tooth contact patterns.
- Many minor alignment problems do not need treatment, but moderate or severe malocclusion can affect chewing, cleaning, enamel wear, and jaw or muscle strain.
- When the record only says "bad bite" or "malocclusion" and does not name the pattern, `K07.4` is the safest bucket.

## Scope note

- Use this note only for unspecified bite problems.
- If the record names crowding, spacing, rotation, impacted teeth, or a specific arch relationship, map to the more specific K07.2 or K07.3 code instead when the source supports it.
- Do not use this bucket for jaw-size problems or temporomandibular disorders.
