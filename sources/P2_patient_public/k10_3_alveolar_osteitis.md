## Verified takeaways

- Dry socket happens when the protective blood clot after extraction is lost too early.
- Bone and nerves can become exposed, causing intense pain and delayed healing.
- Risk factors include smoking, straws, vigorous rinsing, poor oral hygiene, hormonal birth control, and wisdom tooth extraction.
- Dentists often treat it with irrigation, medicated dressings, and pain control.
