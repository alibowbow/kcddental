## Verified takeaways

- Gingival hyperplasia is gum overgrowth that can make teeth look short and harder to clean.
- Causes can include inflammation, medication effects, heredity, and hormone changes.
- Better hygiene and cause correction may help, but some patients need surgical trimming.
