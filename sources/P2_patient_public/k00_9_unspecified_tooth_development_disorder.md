## Verified takeaways

- K00.9 is a fallback code for tooth-development problems when the note does not give enough detail to choose a more specific subtype.
- MedlinePlus shows that tooth-development problems can involve delayed or absent tooth formation, abnormal tooth shape, and abnormal color.
- The same broad workup logic applies: oral exam, family history, associated syndromic findings, and dental X-rays when needed.
- It is safer to treat K00.9 as a documentation placeholder than as a diagnosis with its own distinctive biology.

## Scope note

- Use K00.9 only when the chart truly leaves the subtype unspecified.
- If the note mentions eruption delay, missing teeth, shape, color, enamel, or dentin changes, prefer a more specific K00 code.
- This root should stay broad but shallow, because its job is to hold ambiguous cases until a better subtype is identified.
