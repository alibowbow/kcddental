## Verified takeaways

- K00.8 is best treated as a bucket for specified tooth-development problems that are not delayed eruption or missing teeth.
- Tooth color changes can happen from inherited enamel problems, metabolic conditions, environmental causes, infections, or medicines taken during tooth development.
- Amelogenesis imperfecta and dentinogenesis imperfecta are strong patient-facing examples because they cause abnormal tooth color along with weak enamel or dentin.
- These conditions can also produce pitting, grooves, translucency, rapid wear, sensitivity, cavities, or breakage.

## Scope note

- Keep K00.8 narrower than K00.9.
- Use K00.8 only when the chart actually specifies a developmental tooth problem beyond eruption delay or missing teeth.
- The child codes under K00.8 should be derived conservatively from explicit cause labels such as discoloration from blood incompatibility, biliary disease, porphyria, tetracycline exposure, or another clearly named developmental abnormality.
